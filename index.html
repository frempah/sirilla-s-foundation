Our Story

A Foundation Built on
Love & Purpose

Founded by good hearted individuals who still remembers their root. Serilla's Foundation was born from a simple conviction: no one should face hardship alone. Our founders, now based in the United States, channel their resources and passion back into the African communities to create lasting, meaningful change.

🌱

Pure Intent

Every action we take is driven by genuine love for people, with no hidden agenda.

🤲

Community First

We listen before we act, ensuring our help truly meets needs on the ground.

📈

Sustainable Growth

We plant seeds of change that grow long after we leave a community.

🔍

Transparency

We are fully accountable to our donors, partners, and the people we serve.

Purpose.

What Drives Us Every Day.

🎯

Our Mission.

To provide essential resources, comfort, and dignified support to the needy and less privileged in West Africa, Ghana — addressing hunger, healthcare, education, and shelter — while empowering communities to rise and thrive independently.

🔭

Our Vision.

to create a sustainable community where every person — regardless of background or circumstance — has access to the basics of a dignified life. We envision communities that are self-sustaining, hopeful, and full of opportunity for the next generation.
